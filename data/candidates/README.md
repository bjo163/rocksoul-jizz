# JIZZ Discovery Candidates

> **RAW LEADS / INSPECTED STAGING. NOT PERSPECTIVE TRUTH.**

This directory is the staging area for PERSPECTIVE research before canonical JIZZ promotion.

A candidate is not a canonical SOURCE, OBSERVATION, PHENOMENON, PERSPECTIVE, FRAMING, REACTION, SIGNAL, CHANGE, SNAPSHOT, TREND, PATTERN, or INSIGHT record.

Lifecycle:

```text
discovered
    ↓
triaged
    ↓
needs_sources
    ↓
source_inspected
    ↓
ready_for_observation
    ↓
canonicalized
    ↓
merged
```

A candidate or research envelope may also become `rejected`.

Promotion requires:

1. ISSUE FIRST with Research Issue Contract lineage retained;
2. duplicate check against candidates/index/live data;
3. explicit inspection of the actual source, not only discovery metadata;
4. at least one source suitable for the observation being extracted;
5. provenance-bearing SOURCE + OBSERVATION extraction only after inspection;
6. phenomenon resolution;
7. perspective/framing/reaction extraction only when supported;
8. schema validation, index synchronization, tests, and provenance retention;
9. canon only after lifecycle gates are satisfied.

Scheduler V2 may progress an inspected issue into `ready_for_observation` staging. `needs_sources` candidates must retain `authority: discovery_only`; inspected staging may use `authority: source_inspected` and must include explicit research-issue lineage plus inspection uncertainty.

**ISSUE FIRST. SOURCE SECOND. CANON LAST.**
