# JIZZ Discovery Candidates

> **RAW LEADS. NOT PERSPECTIVE TRUTH.**

This directory is the staging area for automatic perspective research.

A candidate is not a canonical SOURCE, OBSERVATION, PHENOMENON, PERSPECTIVE, FRAMING, or REACTION record.

Lifecycle:

```text
discovered
    ↓
needs_sources
    ↓
ready_for_observation
    ↓
merged
```

A candidate may also become `rejected`.

Promotion requires:

1. duplicate check;
2. inspection of the actual source, not only discovery metadata;
3. at least one source suitable for the observation being extracted;
4. explicit SOURCE + OBSERVATION records;
5. phenomenon resolution;
6. perspective/framing/reaction extraction only when supported;
7. schema validation and provenance retention.

Automatic Scout and Steward stop at `needs_sources`.
