# ROCKSOUL Ecosystem Boundary

`rocksoul-jizz` is the canonical owner of the **PERSPECTIVE** domain.

```text
PERSPECTIVE → rocksoul-jizz → jizz:
```

## Ownership

| Semantic domain | Canonical owner |
|---|---|
| STORY | `rocksoul-mftl` |
| EVENT | `rocksoul-legend` |
| PERSON | `rocksoul-superhero` |
| TEXT | `rocksoul-rgbl` |
| LAW | `rocksoul-aws` |
| **PERSPECTIVE** | **`rocksoul-jizz`** |
| RELATIONSHIP | `rocksoul-correlation` |

JIZZ owns the view, not the observed thing. A local PERSPECTIVE record may reference foreign STORY, EVENT, PERSON, TEXT, LAW, or RELATIONSHIP records without absorbing their ownership.

```text
OBSERVATION ≠ FACT
PERSPECTIVE ≠ TRUTH
FRAMING ≠ STORY
REACTION ≠ EVENT
ACTOR_REFERENCE ≠ PERSON OWNERSHIP
TEXT_REFERENCE ≠ TEXT OWNERSHIP
LEGAL_PERSPECTIVE ≠ LEGAL VERDICT
CORRELATION ≠ JIZZ
```

`rocksoul-correlation` owns reviewed cross-domain relationship edges. JIZZ may describe and analyze perspective states, but it must not mint another repository's canonical record or become a global correlation graph.

MIZAN remains an analytical methodology/capability. JIZZ results are not universal Mizan verdicts.
