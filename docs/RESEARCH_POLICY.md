# JIZZ Research Policy

## Mission

Observe how phenomena are seen, framed, discussed, and reacted to without converting public discourse into canonical truth about the underlying world object.

## Source priority

For canonical observations, prefer:

1. original/primary statements or official publications when the perspective belongs to that actor;
2. original publisher article or transcript;
3. reputable institutional, scientific, academic, or journalistic sources;
4. community/social material when the community reaction itself is the research object;
5. discovery aggregators only as leads.

GDELT metadata is `discovery_only`.

## Mandatory provenance

Every accepted observation should retain:

- source identity;
- stable locator;
- publication time when available;
- retrieval/observation time;
- language;
- geography when available;
- actor/subject references when supported;
- the exact observation type;
- provenance/research run.

## Perspective safety

A perspective record answers **how an actor/source frames a phenomenon**, not whether that actor is correct.

Do not infer a perspective from nationality, ethnicity, religion, political identity, or demographic membership alone.

## Sampling limits

Missing sources, languages, communities, or geographies are uncertainty. Article volume is not automatically public support. Publisher coverage is not population opinion.

## Automation rule

Scheduled automation may create issues and `needs_sources` candidates. Canonical extraction requires source inspection.

## Cross-domain rule

EVENT, PERSON, STORY, TEXT, LAW, and reviewed cross-domain RELATIONSHIP stay with their owning ROCKSOUL repositories.
