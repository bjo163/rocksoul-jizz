```text
JIZZ
```

The architectural law is:

> **JIZZ OWNS THE VIEW. NOT THE THING.**

JIZZ records observation, perspective, framing, reaction, signal, and change around a phenomenon. It does not establish the canonical reality of the phenomenon.

## Perspective is the center

A perspective is an observed way of seeing, interpreting, positioning, or framing a phenomenon. It may carry actor type, zone, geography, audience scope, framing, position, salience, temporal validity, observation references, and provenance.

A perspective is not a correctness judgment.

## Phenomenon

A phenomenon is the subject around which observations cluster. It can be a public controversy, technology launch, cultural movement, scientific controversy, geopolitical discourse, economic concern, or another subject of perception.

A phenomenon may point to a canonical EVENT, PERSON, STORY, TEXT, LAW, or reviewed correlation externally, but it does not replace those records.

## Actor

Actor references remain observational. Actor types include MEDIA, GOVERNMENT, CORPORATE, SCIENTIFIC, ACADEMIC, COMMUNITY, CREATOR, ACTIVIST, PUBLIC, INVESTOR, LEGAL, CULTURAL, TECHNICAL, LOCAL, GLOBAL, and UNKNOWN.

A named actor can reference a canonical PERSON when one exists, but JIZZ does not mint person identity merely because a name appears.

## Temporal rule

Perspectives are append-only historical observations. A later perspective does not overwrite an earlier one. `valid_from` / `valid_until` express a perspective's modeled validity interval while `observed_at`, `published_at`, and `retrieved_at` retain acquisition chronology.

## Golden rules

```text
OBSERVATION ≠ FACT
PERSPECTIVE ≠ TRUTH
FRAMING ≠ STORY
REACTION ≠ EVENT
SIGNAL ≠ FACT
TREND ≠ HISTORICAL TRUTH
CORRELATION ≠ JIZZ
```
