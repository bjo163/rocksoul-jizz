# Vocabulary

## Core

**SOURCE** — origin of an observation; not truth.

**OBSERVATION** — something observed from a source at a point in time; not a canonical fact claim.

**PHENOMENON** — subject around which observations and perspectives cluster.

**PERSPECTIVE** — observed way of seeing/interpreting/positioning a phenomenon.

**FRAMING** — angle from which a phenomenon is presented.

**POSITION** — SUPPORT, OPPOSE, NEUTRAL, MIXED, QUESTIONING, or UNKNOWN. Position is observational, not correctness.

**REACTION** — observed response such as support, criticism, rejection, amplification, satire, adoption, warning, or correction.

**SIGNAL** — derived pattern from observations.

**CHANGE** — measured difference between temporal states.

## Derived

**PERSPECTIVE_SNAPSHOT** — reconstructable state of the perspective field at a timestamp.

**TREND** — derived state: EMERGING, RISING, PEAKING, STABLE, DECLINING, RESURGING, or DORMANT.

**PATTERN** — detected shift type with explicit inputs.

**INSIGHT** — explainable derived intelligence with methodology and lineage.

## JIZZ behavior

**JUXTAPOSE** — compare angles side-by-side and expose difference, overlap, distance, conflict, and convergence.

**ZIGZAG** — model how perspective/framing/reaction/attention/actor/geography shifts over time.

**ZONE** — contextual metadata such as GLOBAL, REGIONAL, LOCAL, DIGITAL, CULTURAL, TECHNICAL, POLITICAL, ECONOMIC, SCIENTIFIC, or COMMUNITY.

## Secondary dimensions

Sentiment may be POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, NEUTRAL, MIXED, or UNKNOWN. Sentiment is secondary and must not collapse the perspective model into sentiment analysis.
